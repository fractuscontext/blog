The ones who see cyberspace differently.

They're not fond of usability guidelines, and they have absolutely no respect for W3C compliance.

You can ping them, leave comments on their walls, flame their ideas, or greet them as old friends.

About the only thing you can't do is ignore their homepage because their autoplaying MIDI files make that impossible.

Because the people who are crazy enough to welcome the whole world into their place in cyberspace, are the ones who make it feel like home.
